At AK Design Studio, we offer complete design and consultancy services to support your project. Our Vaastu consulting service integrates ancient principles of harmony and balance into contemporary design, helping clients create spaces that nurture well-being and prosperity. For those looking to refresh an existing property, our remodeling service handles everything from structural changes to cosmetic transformations with minimal disruption.
Our interior design offering brings spaces to life through thoughtful space planning, material selection, and finishing tailored to reflect your personal style and vision.
We also manage the full planning and approvals process on your behalf, handling documentation and liaising with local authorities. And with decades of experience across hotels, residences, and large-scale turnkey developments, our construction consultancy ensures every build is delivered on time, on budget, and to the highest standard.
